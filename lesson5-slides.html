What Is a Strip Diagram?

A strip diagram is a bar that we split into parts to show a problem.

?

The whole bar stands for the total. We break it into pieces to show what we know and what we're looking for.

Ask Zoey:

  • "What do you think the bar represents in a problem?"

One Step vs. Two Steps

One-step: Sam has 5 apples and gets 3 more. How many now?

5 + 3 = 8

Two-step: Sam has 5 apples, gets 3 more, then gives away 4. How many now?

5 + 3 = 8   →   8 − 4 = 4

What Is a Remainder?

When we divide and numbers don't split evenly, the leftover amount is the remainder.

13 ÷ 4 = 3 remainder 1

4 groups of 3, with 1 left over.

Ask Zoey:

  • "Can you show 13 split into groups of 4 with counters or drawings?"

Interpreting Remainders: Round Up

29 kids are going on a field trip. Each van holds 8 kids. How many vans are needed?

29 ÷ 8 = 3 remainder 5 → need 4 vans

Even though the remainder is small, we still need a whole extra van!

Ask Zoey:

  • "Why can't we leave those 5 kids without a van?"

Interpreting Remainders: Drop It

Zoey has 29 stickers and wants to put them in bags of 8. How many full bags can she make?

29 ÷ 8 = 3 remainder 5 → only 3 full bags

Here, the leftover 5 doesn't make a full bag, so we drop it.

Ask Zoey:

  • "How is this different from the van problem?"

Try It Together

Zoey earns $6 per chore. She does 4 chores, then spends $9 on a toy. How much money does she have left?

4 × 6 = 24   →   24 − 9 = 15
