The Area Model

Let's solve 23 × 4 using an area model — a rectangle split into two easier pieces.

20 × 4
= 80
3 × 4
= 12
80 + 12 = 92

Writing Out Partial Products

The two smaller multiplication problems from the area model are called partial products.

20 × 4 = 80  (partial product 1)
3 × 4 = 12  (partial product 2)
80 + 12 = 92

Connecting to the Standard Algorithm

The standard algorithm does the same math, just written a different way.

  23
×  4
  92

Multiply the ones (3 × 4 = 12, write 2, carry the 1), then multiply the tens (2 × 4 = 8, plus the carried 1 = 9).
