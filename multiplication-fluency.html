💡 Strategy Corner (for tricky facts)
  • Doubles: 2s are just doubling. For 4s, double twice. For 8s, double three times.
  • 5s: Count by 5s — answers always end in 0 or 5.
  • 9s: Do ×10, then subtract one group. 9 × 7 → 70 − 7 = 63.
  • Break it apart: 7 × 8 → (7 × 5) + (7 × 3) = 35 + 21 = 56. This is the same area-model idea from Week 9!
  • 11s: Do ×10, then add one group. 11 × 6 → 60 + 6 = 66.
  • Flip it: If 3 × 8 is easier than 8 × 3, they have the same answer!
