Rounding to the nearest ten, hundred & thousand — mixed with expanded notation up to the ten-thousands place

Part 1: Rounding Review

The Golden Rule

Look one place to the RIGHT of what you're rounding to

0, 1, 2, 3, 4 → round DOWN (that place stays the same)

5, 6, 7, 8, 9 → round UP (that place goes up by one)

Every digit after the rounding place becomes a zero.

Rounding to the Nearest Thousand

Round 42,637 to the nearest thousand.

42,637 rounds to 43,000

Look at the hundreds digit (6). Since 6 ≥ 5, round the thousands digit up from 2 to 3, and everything after becomes 0.

Rounding to the Nearest Hundred

15,342

Look at the tens digit: 4. Since 4 < 5, round down — the hundreds digit stays the same.

15,342 rounds to 15,300

Rounding to the Nearest Ten

7,835

Look at the ones digit: 5. Since 5 ≥ 5, round up — the tens digit goes from 3 to 4.

7,835 rounds to 7,840

❌ Watch Out!

Rounding 15,342 to the nearest hundred → 15,000 ❌
15,342 rounds to 15,300 ✔️

Only round to the place that was asked for — don't accidentally round all the way to the thousands!
